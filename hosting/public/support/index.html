Account and sign-in

Who can sign up?

Sign-up uses a university email address: @ogr.gumushane.edu.tr (students) or @gumushane.edu.tr (staff). During the test period, Gmail addresses are temporarily accepted as well. Your password must be at least 8 characters long and contain at least one capital letter and one number.

I didn’t receive the verification email

  1. On the “Verify your email” screen, check that your address is spelled correctly. If not, fix it with Change email.
  2. Look in the Junk / Spam folder of your mailbox. The email can take a few minutes to arrive.
  3. If it still hasn’t arrived, tap Resend (available once every 60 seconds).
  4. After clicking the link in the email, return to the app and tap I verified it.

If the problem continues, write to us and include the address you signed up with.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password, enter the email address you signed up with and tap Send link. Set a new password with the link in the email. For security, the same confirmation is shown even if the address is not registered. After 5 failed sign-in attempts in a row you need to wait 30 seconds.

How do I change my password?

Profile › Settings › Change password.

Can I change my email address?

The email address you signed up with identifies your account and cannot be changed later. If you mistyped it during verification, use Change email on the verification screen.

Clubs and applications

How do I apply to a club?

Open the club from the Clubs tab and tap Join. For clubs that require approval you write a short note to the club management (up to 300 characters; only club management and the app administrators can see it). For instant-join clubs you become a member right away. You can follow the result in Notifications.

Why is my application still pending?

Applications are reviewed by the club’s board. You can see the status in Profile › My clubs & applications › Pending, where you can also update your note or cancel the request.

My application was rejected. Can I apply again?

Yes. If your application was rejected or you were removed from a club, you can apply again after 7 days; the app shows the time remaining.

My club isn’t in the app. How can it be added?

Clubs are added by the app administrators. The club’s president or advisor can email us with the club’s name.

Events and QR tickets

How do I register for an event?

Tap Join on the event page. If the event is full, tap Join waitlist; when a spot opens you are moved up automatically in order and get a notification. Members-only events are open only to the club’s members. If you can’t attend, tap Leave before the event ends so your spot goes to someone on the waitlist.

Where is my ticket?

Tap Show ticket on the event page or open Profile › My tickets. At the entrance, staff scan the QR code on your ticket to check you in.

My QR code won’t scan. What should I do?

Raise the screen brightness and open the ticket full screen. If it still fails, read the code under the QR to the staff; it can be entered manually.

Notifications

I’m not getting notifications

  • Check that notifications are allowed for GÜ Kulüpler in your phone’s settings.
  • In Profile › Settings › Notification preferences, make sure the relevant notification types are not turned off.
  • If you muted a club, you won’t get notifications from it.
  • During quiet hours, notifications arrive silently.

Reporting and blocking

How do I report content or a user?

Use the ⋯ menu on a post, comment, user or club, choose Report, pick a reason (Spam, Inappropriate content, Harassment or bullying, Misinformation, Not relevant to the club, Other) and add a short note if you like. Reports are reviewed by the app administrators and you are notified of the outcome. Your identity is not shown to the person reported. You can send up to 20 reports per day.

How do I block a user?

Use Block user in the ⋯ menu of their post, or Block on their profile. You will no longer see their posts and comments, and they are not notified.

How do I unblock someone?

Profile › Settings › Blocked users.

Account and data deletion

How do I delete my account?

  1. Open Profile › Settings › Delete account and read the warnings. If you are a club president, you must hand over the presidency first.
  2. Optionally choose why you are leaving (this is not stored).
  3. Enter your password, type SİL to confirm and tap Permanently delete my account.

Your name becomes “Deleted user”; your profile details, email address, memberships, pending applications and upcoming event tickets are removed. Your posts and comments remain, shown as “Deleted user”. This cannot be undone, and you can no longer sign in with the same account. Details: Privacy Policy, section 7.

How do I request full deletion of my data?

Some records remain after account deletion (for example your sign-in record, posts and comments, past event attendance and support requests). To have them permanently deleted, email eminalan334@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject “Data deletion request”. You can also make this request without deleting your account first. We will answer within 30 days at the latest.

Other

Is this the university’s official app?

No. GÜ Kulüpler is an independent student project built by students; it is not developed, operated or endorsed by Gümüşhane University.

Which languages does the app support?

Turkish and English. You can change the language in Profile › Settings › Language.

How is my data used?

Our Privacy Policy and KVKK Notice explains what data is collected, who can see it and what your rights are. The app has no ads, tracking, analytics tools or location data.
